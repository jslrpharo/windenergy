Real-Time Simulators — Digital Twins

In Real-Time Simulators you will find Digital Twins specialised by wind turbine family — unique in the market. They let you experience their behaviour as what they truly are: electricity production plants designed for unattended operation. Observe the turbine's reaction under normal conditions, exceptional scenarios (storm winds, grid faults, extreme temperatures) and with component failures, as if you were inside the wind turbine. Each simulator is accompanied by interactive web tutorials explaining every component in detail.

Tutorials & Practical Exercises

The tutorials will complement your own teaching material with details that are hard to find elsewhere: cutaway diagrams, annotated signal traces, and cross-referenced fault scenarios. A set of Practical Exercises is also included so that students can reproduce situations similar to those that actually occur in real wind turbine operation — from normal start/stop sequences to complex fault diagnosis.

Drive Train Analysis & B2B Converters

Two key applications for wind turbine design: the Drive Train Analysis Software (gearbox dynamics, mechanical transients, torsional stress) and the tool for studying DFIG Back-to-Back Converters (grid-side and rotor-side converter, PWM modulation, active and reactive power flows).
